FAQ

Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free. Booking guarantees your time, especially on Fridays and Saturdays.

How far ahead should I book?

A few days is usually plenty. For Saturdays or a specific barber, book about a week ahead.

What if I’m running late or need to cancel?

Just call or message us. We’ll fit you in if we can. Please give us 24 hours’ notice to cancel.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes, for all ages. Kids cuts (under 12) are £15.

How can I pay?

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash.

Is there parking?

Free on-street parking on Barber Street, and a pay-and-display car park two minutes away.
